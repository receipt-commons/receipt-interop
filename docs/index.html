receipt-interop demo

One library, three receipt formats. Press verify and the page checks each sample receipt live in your browser — real cryptography, no server, nothing faked. Built on receipt-interop 0.1.0.

authproof draft JWS profile: compact JWS, ES256, JCS-canonical payload.
